How to Get a DUNS Number for Google Play Developer Account

TH
12 Testers Hub Team
Updated: This Week • 7 min read

If you are a solo developer in the US or UK, you have probably looked at the 14-day closed testing rule and wondered: "Is there a legal way to skip this?"

Yes, there is. If you register an Organization Developer Account instead of a Personal Account, Google exempts you from the 12-tester requirement. But to prove you are a real business, Google requires a 9-digit D-U-N-S number.

If you are asking, "How do I get a DUNS number for Google Play?"—here is everything you need to know, including the timeline, the cost, and why it might not be the magic bullet you think it is.

What is a DUNS Number?

DUNS stands for Data Universal Numbering System. It is a proprietary system developed and managed by Dun & Bradstreet (D&B), a massive corporate data analytics company. A DUNS number uniquely identifies a physical business entity on a global scale.

Google uses DUNS verification to ensure that fake developers cannot simply spin up a new email address to bypass the Play Console's quality guidelines. If you have a DUNS number, it means your business is legally registered with your local government.

How to Get a DUNS Number (Step-by-Step)

Before you start this process, you must already have a registered LLC, Corporation, or Partnership. If you are operating as a sole proprietor without a registered business entity, you cannot get a DUNS number.

Step 1: Register Your Business

If you haven't already, register your LLC with your state (in the US) or register with Companies House (in the UK). This gives you the official Articles of Incorporation that D&B will need to verify.

Step 2: Visit the Dun & Bradstreet Portal

You can request a DUNS number directly through the D&B website. Google actually provides a specific link inside the Play Console when you try to switch your account to an Organization. Using that link is the safest route.

Step 3: Submit Your Business Details

You will need to provide your legal business name, physical address (no P.O. boxes), your business registration number, and the name of a principal authority (like the CEO or Managing Member).

Step 4: Wait for Verification

D&B will cross-reference your details with public government registries. If everything matches, they will issue your 9-digit DUNS number.

How Much Does it Cost?

This is the good news: Requesting a standard DUNS number for the purpose of app development or government grants is completely free.

However, D&B will try to upsell you aggressively on "credit building" packages or expedited services. Do not buy them. Google Play only requires the standard, free 9-digit number.

The Catch: The 30-Day Timeline

Here is why getting a DUNS number is not a quick fix for impatient developers. While the number is free, the verification process takes up to 30 days (sometimes longer in the UK).

Once you finally receive the number, you still have to submit it to Google Play, which takes another few days to verify on their end. If your app is ready to launch today, waiting a month for a DUNS number is often worse than just doing the 14-day closed test.

Pro Tip: If you already have a DUNS number because you registered an LLC months ago, the process is instant. You just plug the number into the Play Console, and your Organization account is approved within 48 hours.
