Google Play closed testing FAQ

What is the 12 testers for 14 days rule?

New personal Google Play developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days before applying for production access.

Can I use my own accounts as testers?

No. Testers must be distinct, real Google accounts on separate devices. Duplicate or self-owned accounts risk rejection or suspension.

How fast do the 12 testers join?

Usually within 12–24 hours after you share your closed testing opt-in link.

Is it safe for my developer account?

Our testers are real people on real Android devices who opt in through your official testing link, the same way real users do. No bots and no emulators.

What if a tester uninstalls before day 14?

We check retention daily and replace any tester who drops off at no extra cost.

How does multi-app pricing work?

1 app costs $23 and every app includes 12 testers. Adding apps lowers the price per app: 2 apps $43, 3 apps $58, 4 apps $70, 5 apps $81.
