Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

12TestersHub.com ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

When you fill out our order form, we collect:

  • Personal Information: Your full name and email address.
  • Contact Information: Your WhatsApp number to coordinate the testing process.
  • App Data: Your Google Play closed testing opt-in link.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your testing service order.
  • To communicate with you regarding your tester status and 14-day retention progress.
  • To provide support regarding the Google Play Production Access Questionnaire.

Data Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your app links and data are kept strictly confidential and are only used to complete the testing requirement.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via WhatsApp.
